Distance, area, and angle measurements depend entirely on accurate calibration and careful manual tracing; treat them as estimates, not clinical-grade measurements. Echo intensity is the mean grayscale value (0 to 255) of the original image pixels within the drawn rectangle or area shape (drawn lines and shapes are never included). Color pixels are converted with luminance weights (0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B); for grayscale images this equals the pixel value, the same as ImageJ's mean gray value. No depth-attenuation correction is applied. Pennation angle is the acute angle between the fascicle line and the aponeurosis line you draw.
