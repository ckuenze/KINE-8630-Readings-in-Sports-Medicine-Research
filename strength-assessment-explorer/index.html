Simulated data for teaching purposes, not real patient recordings. Average RTD is computed over the middle 60% of the onset-to-peak window; torque steadiness is computed over the middle 1 second of the 3-second contraction (both anchored to the detected onset, not a hidden ground truth). Peak search is bounded to the first second after onset to avoid picking up a random noise excursion late in the plateau — a real methodological choice, not just a simulation convenience.
